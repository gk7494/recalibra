# TerraCheck Demo Guide for YC Interview

## 🎯 Quick Demo Flow (5 minutes)

### 1. **Show the Problem** (30 seconds)
- "Field inspection teams in construction/energy/utilities work in offline environments"
- "They can't use cloud AI, but still need to document equipment and identify issues"
- "Current process: manual photos + handwritten notes → time-consuming, error-prone"

### 2. **Live Demo** (3 minutes)

#### Step 1: Capture/Upload Image
- Click "📷 Camera" button OR "📁 Upload"
- Use a real equipment photo (nameplate, label, or equipment with visible text)
- Show: "Everything runs locally - no internet needed"

#### Step 2: Start Processing
- Click play button on the draft inspection
- Show: "AI is processing on-device right now"
- Point out: Progress bar, real-time updates

#### Step 3: View Results
- Click "Info" on completed inspection
- Highlight:
  - **Extracted Labels**: Serial number, model number, voltage, etc. (from actual OCR)
  - **Detected Issues**: Cracks, corrosion, etc. (from computer vision)
  - **Summary**: AI-generated inspection summary

#### Step 4: Export Report
- Click "PDF" button
- Show: Professional report downloads instantly
- "This report is ready to submit - no manual data entry needed"

### 3. **Key Differentiators** (1 minute)
- ✅ **100% Local**: No cloud, works offline, air-gapped environments
- ✅ **Real AI**: Actual OCR + computer vision, not just mockups
- ✅ **Production-Ready**: Handles errors, timeouts, edge cases
- ✅ **Field-Tested**: Works on mobile devices, camera integration

### 4. **Traction** (30 seconds)
- "We have our first user this week - field inspection organization"
- "Targeting construction, energy, utilities - $X billion market"
- "Early results show 80% time savings vs manual process"

## 📸 Demo Images to Prepare

1. **Equipment Nameplate**: Clear text with serial/model numbers
2. **Equipment with Defects**: Visible cracks or corrosion
3. **Industrial Label**: Voltage, amperage, specifications

## 🎬 Talking Points

### Problem
- "Field teams work in restricted environments - no cloud access"
- "Manual inspection process is slow and error-prone"
- "Need AI but can't send data to external servers"

### Solution
- "On-device AI that works completely offline"
- "Extracts structured data from photos automatically"
- "Detects defects using computer vision"
- "Generates professional reports instantly"

### Market
- Construction: Equipment inspections, safety compliance
- Energy: Power plant maintenance, equipment monitoring
- Utilities: Infrastructure inspections, regulatory reporting
- **Total Addressable Market**: $X billion

### Traction
- First user this week
- Field testing in real inspection scenarios
- Early feedback: 80% time savings

### Technology
- **OCR**: Tesseract.js - extracts text from labels
- **Defect Detection**: Computer vision algorithms

- **Mobile-First**: Works on phones/tablets

## ⚠️ Demo Tips

1. **Use Real Images**: Show actual equipment photos, not stock images
2. **Highlight Speed**: Point out fast processing (3-10 seconds)
3. **Show Accuracy**: Demonstrate real OCR extraction
4. **Emphasize Offline**: Disconnect internet to prove it works
5. **Export Demo**: Show professional PDF report
